隐私政策 — 邮箱留痕

最后更新：2026 年 5 月 6 日

概述

邮箱留痕（MailTrace）是一款本地化的照片时间水印与邮件归档工具。我们高度重视您的隐私。

本应用不收集、不存储、不传输任何用户个人数据。

数据处理

  • 照片处理：所有照片的水印添加和压缩均在您的设备本地完成，不会上传到任何服务器。
  • 邮件发送：通过 iOS 系统邮件客户端发送，应用本身不接触、不存储您的邮箱密码。
  • 邮箱地址：您设置的收件邮箱仅保存在设备本地（UserDefaults），不会传输到外部。
  • 水印设置：自定义水印文字仅保存在设备本地。

权限使用

  • 相机：仅用于拍摄照片，照片处理完全在本地进行。
  • 照片库：通过系统选择器访问，应用无法浏览您的完整相册。

第三方服务

本应用不集成任何第三方分析、广告或追踪 SDK。

数据存储

应用仅在设备本地存储以下偏好设置：

  • 收件邮箱地址（最多 3 个）
  • 水印后缀文字

卸载应用后，以上数据将被完全删除。

儿童隐私

本应用不针对 13 岁以下儿童，不主动收集任何人的个人信息。

Privacy Policy — MailTrace

Last updated: May 6, 2026

Overview

MailTrace is a local photo timestamp watermarking and email archiving tool. We take your privacy seriously.

This app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal user data.

Data Processing

  • Photo processing: All watermarking and compression happens entirely on your device. No photos are uploaded to any server.
  • Email sending: Emails are sent through the iOS system mail client. The app never accesses or stores your email password.
  • Email addresses: Recipient addresses you configure are stored only in local device storage (UserDefaults) and are never transmitted externally.
  • Watermark settings: Custom watermark text is stored only on your device.

Permissions

  • Camera: Used solely for taking photos. All processing occurs locally.
  • Photo Library: Accessed via the system photo picker. The app cannot browse your full photo library.

Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Data Storage

The app stores only the following preferences locally on your device:

  • Recipient email addresses (up to 3)
  • Watermark suffix text

All data is completely removed when the app is uninstalled.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
